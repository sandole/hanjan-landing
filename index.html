Welcome to Hanjan

한잔 : A Drink

Welcome to Hanjan, where traditional Korean comfort food meets warm hospitality. Family-owned and operated by a mother-and-son duo since 2017, we serve freshly prepared Korean favorites made with authentic ingredients and care. From our signature Korean fried chicken and bulgogi fries to hearty stews, japchae, and sizzling samgyupsal, there’s something for every occasion. Visit us at our four Edmonton locations in Oliver (Downtown), Southside, Whyte Avenue, and West for a welcoming meal from lunch through late night.

A mother and son kitchen

Proudly family-owned since 2017, Hanjan brings together traditional Korean home cooking and Canadian influences. Every dish is prepared with fresh, authentic ingredients and care, right down to our house-made kimchi. More than a place to enjoy comforting Korean food, Hanjan is a welcoming gathering spot where family, friends, and community come together over meals made with love.

Korean fried chicken

Our Korean fried chicken is marinated for 24 hours, double-battered, and served in original, Padak, or Yangyum styles. Pair your meal with locally roasted coffee, teas, or refreshing soju cocktails, including somaek, Korea’s popular soju-and-beer mix. Finish on a sweet note with our famous Honey Butter Bread or refreshing bingsoo desserts for a complete Hanjan experience.
